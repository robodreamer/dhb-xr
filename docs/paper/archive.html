Positioning

DHB-XR is best understood as an action-side representation layer. It composes with tokenizers and policies rather than replacing them, and its main value is making trajectory identity stable under workspace changes.

vs relative actions: Per-step deltas are locally frame-invariant, but the whole sequence still changes when the scene moves. DHB preserves the global motion pattern.

vs FAST / OAT / ActionCodec: These compress raw coordinates efficiently but inherit frame sensitivity. DHB-XR makes those action targets spatially stable before tokenization.

vs equivariant or 3D-aware policies: Those approaches act on the network/perception side. DHB-XR is orthogonal because it changes the action representation itself.
