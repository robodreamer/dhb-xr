\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Every current Vision-Language-Action model predicts actions in a world
coordinate frame: absolute end-effector positions and orientations relative
to the robot's base or camera.
This seems natural --- but it encodes a fundamental assumption:
that the coordinate frame of training is the same as the coordinate frame of deployment.

\textbf{This assumption is wrong, and it is the primary source of VLA brittleness.}
When the robot base shifts, the table height changes, or an object is placed
$10\,\text{cm}$ to the left, the coordinate frame changes --- and every predicted
action becomes incorrect, regardless of model capacity.
The evidence is consistent: OpenVLA-OFT degrades $-17.5\%$,
StarVLA $-18.1\%$, ACoT-VLA $-14.4\%$ under spatial distribution shifts.
More data and larger models cannot fix this: it is a \emph{representational problem}.

\textbf{We propose \emph{invariant manipulation learning}:}
design action representations invariant to the choice of coordinate frame,
and handle spatial variation through principled geometric operations.

\dhb{} instantiates this principle via the SE(3) quotient of motion-shape modulo pose.
Magnitude tokens are provably invariant ($0.0\%$ change under SE(3) transforms vs $99.1\%$
for raw actions).
A $\leq\!7\,\text{ms}$ adaptation step re-grounds invariant shape to any target pose.
The result: zero-shot spatial generalization from $N{=}1$ demonstration,
$0.0\,\text{mm}$ error at $10\,\text{cm}$ perturbation, across an entire pose neighborhood
without retraining.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Invariant spatial generalization.}
$0.0\,\text{mm}$ goal error at $10\,\text{cm}$ perturbation vs $100.0\,\text{mm}$ naive.
$N{=}1$ demo sufficient for any pose; same result at $N{=}20$.

\item \textbf{Provably invariant action tokenization.}
$0.0\%$ magnitude token change under SE(3); 8-token OAT-style with prefix decodability.
First action tokenizer with a geometric invariance guarantee.

\item \textbf{Sample efficiency via invariant retrieval.}
One invariant-encoded demo spans the entire pose neighborhood of a task.

\item \textbf{Cross-embodiment.}
Invariant trajectories are robot-agnostic; decoded via embodik IK to any manipulator.

\item \textbf{Modular ablation infrastructure.}
\texttt{DHBActionHeader} for StarVLA; clean comparison of invariant vs non-invariant heads.
\end{enumerate}

This work establishes SE(3)-invariance as a first principle for manipulation learning:
not a regularizer, not a data augmentation, but a structural guarantee built into
the representation itself.
